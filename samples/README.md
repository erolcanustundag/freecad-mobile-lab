# FreeCAD Mobile Lab candidate examples

Five exact CAD projects and STL exports: 18 mm comfort ring, signet with a circular seat, pendant frame, editable plate and loft with a separate weighted curve. The plate project demonstrates an edited hidden Rectangle root at 24 × 12 mm, padded 3 mm with a radius-2 mm hole. The in-app plate template initially uses 20 × 12 mm. Loft STL contains the solid; its curve is retained in the project.

Two graph projects and .cadgraph recipes demonstrate a parametric ring and an eight-bezel linear pattern. Project files use schema v2 and need a candidate reader; old 0.9 cannot open them.

A4 sheets include 20 × 12 mm and 400 × 300 mm profiles, with overall dimensions. The large sheet scales down; print both at 100%, no page fit. Native contours use bounded sampling; dimension labels use CAD bounds.

These are reference fixtures, not manufacturing-certified designs. Print/casting calibration and Android round trips remain pending. Mass/casting percentages require your own alloy/process measurements.
